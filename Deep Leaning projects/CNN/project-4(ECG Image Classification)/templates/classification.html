Quick facts
    Summary

    This tool is for research and educational purposes only and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician for diagnosis and treatment.

    Tips for better predictions

    • Use clear, well-lit ECG images without shadows or heavy compression artifacts.
    • Crop to the ECG panel if the photo includes background clutter.
    • Avoid rotations or extreme perspective distortion.
    • When possible, export a digital image directly from the ECG machine.

    About

    This model classifies ECG images into four classes: Myocardial Infarction, History of MI, Abnormal Heartbeat, and Normal. Results are instant and paired with helpful explanations.
